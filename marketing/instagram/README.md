# ParleSprint Instagram Content Calendar

This folder contains the ready-to-post Instagram calendar for ParleSprint, a French-learning app for people prepping for TCF Canada / TEF Canada exams (NCLC/CLB 7+ for CRS points).

## Files

- `instagram_calendar.json`, 39 posts, one object per post, ready to copy into a scheduler or post manually.

## Cadence

Monday, Wednesday, Friday at 17:00, starting 2026-07-20 and running through 2026-10-16. That is 39 posts across four phases:

1. Pre-launch teaser (2026-07-20 to 2026-08-07). Value-first content, exam-format explainers, myths, vocab, light founder personality. No app name mentioned until the last stretch of this phase.
2. Launch window (2026-08-10 to 2026-08-12). Beta opens, announced low-key, still value-first tone.
3. Beta era (2026-08-14 to 2026-09-09). Exam tips, small user wins, founder moments, occasional soft app mentions.
4. Founding member era (2026-09-11 to 2026-10-16). Founding member offer mentioned occasionally, testimonials once available, still mostly value content.

## Promo ratio

Roughly 1 in 5 posts mentions the app or the founding member offer directly. The rest are pure French-learning value or founder personality content, with no product pitch. This ratio is intentional: the account should feel useful to follow even for someone who never downloads the app. Direct promo posts cluster naturally around the launch dates and the founding member announcement, that is expected and fine, just do not let promo density creep upward outside those windows.

## Tone guidance

- No em dashes, no double hyphens used as a dash. Periods and commas instead.
- No AI-sounding phrasing: avoid "swipe to learn more," "let's dive in," forced enthusiasm, identical caption openers.
- Vary rhythm on purpose: some captions are one line, some are a short story, some are a direct tip with a casual aside.
- Never claim IRCC, TCF, or TEF endorsement. Never guarantee a score or outcome.
- Testimonial posts (2026-09-18 and 2026-10-09) use placeholder-but-realistic user quotes. Swap in real collected testimonials before those dates if available, otherwise keep the framing general and honest.
- Keep the founder voice consistent: a real person building this alone, transparent about bugs and unglamorous work, not a polished brand voice.
