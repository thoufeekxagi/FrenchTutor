# ParleSprint Twitter/X Calendar

## What this is

A 90 day calendar of daily tweets for building ParleSprint in public, from 2026-07-20
through 2026-10-17. ParleSprint is an app that helps people prepare for the TCF Canada
and TEF Canada exams used in Canadian immigration applications, targeting NCLC/CLB 7+
for CRS points.

All 90 entries live in `twitter_calendar.json`. Each entry is one day, one tweet
(occasional short threads are marked with `is_thread: true` and the follow up tweet
in `thread_continuation`).

## Philosophy

This is not a marketing campaign, it's a running log of actually building something.
The account exists to:

- Document real progress, real bugs, real decisions, in public, as they happen.
- Talk to future users where they already are, people prepping for TCF/TEF and
  thinking about their CRS score, not a generic "language learners" audience.
- Build trust before asking for money. The paid founding member tier doesn't show
  up until roughly day 70, after weeks of visible beta iteration and real user
  feedback.
- Stay honest. No fake urgency, no guaranteed scores, no claiming any endorsement
  from IRCC, TCF, or TEF. Just what was actually built, what broke, and what a real
  user said.

Content rotates across a handful of types so the feed doesn't feel like a template:
progress updates, feature explainers, lessons learned (including mistakes), questions
to the audience, behind the scenes founder moments, milestones and numbers, French
exam tips tied to the app's angle, and the occasional lighter engagement tweet. No
single format repeats two days in a row for long stretches, and no two tweets share
an opening line.

## Posting time

Every tweet posts at **08:00** local time, once a day. Consistency matters more than
timing optimization at this stage, same time every morning, so it becomes a habit for
both the founder and anyone following along.

## Budget

This whole effort is meant to fit inside about 20 minutes a day: paste the scheduled
tweet, reply to anything that came in overnight, done. Mini threads are rare (a small
handful across the full 90 days) and only used when a genuine milestone needs more
than 280 characters.

## Real milestones the calendar tracks

- Days 1 to 21: finishing the core build, placement test, first units, speaking and
  writing feedback loop, pre-launch prep.
- Around day 22 to 24: beta opens to an email waitlist.
- Days 25 to 56: beta feedback, iteration, TCF/TEF style mock exam tasks, first
  testimonials.
- Days 57 to 90: founding member paid tier launches (~$49/year), first paying users,
  first real progress stories, planning what's next.
