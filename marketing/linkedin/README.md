# ParleSprint LinkedIn Calendar

This folder contains the LinkedIn content calendar for ParleSprint, a French learning app for people preparing for TCF Canada / TEF Canada exams as part of their Express Entry / CRS points strategy.

## File

`linkedin_calendar.json` contains 26 ready-to-post entries, each with:

- `date`, `time`, `day_of_week`
- `phase` (1, 2, or 3, see below)
- `topic`, an internal short label
- `text`, the full post copy, ready to paste into LinkedIn
- `notes`, a short internal reminder, often empty

## Cadence

Posts go out Tuesdays and Fridays at 10:00, from 2026-07-21 through 2026-10-16.

## Phases

1. Build and pre-launch (2026-07-21 to 2026-08-07): the why behind the product, problems with existing French apps for this specific audience, founder reflections, market observations.
2. Launch and beta (2026-08-11 to 2026-09-08): going live, beta learnings, user conversations, RCIC conversations, numbers updates, iteration stories.
3. Founding member tier and traction (2026-09-11 to 2026-10-16): opening the paid tier, first testimonials, gratitude, reflections on traction, and what is next.

## Audience

A mix of three groups reading the same feed: people preparing for Canadian PR who might use the app, immigration consultants (RCICs) who work adjacent to this space, and people following the founder journey of building a product in public.

## Tone guidance

- First person, human, reflective. A notch more professional than Reddit or Twitter, but not corporate.
- No em dashes or double hyphens as a substitute for a comma or period. Use periods or commas instead.
- Avoid AI-sounding phrases like "in today's competitive landscape," "thrilled to announce," "game-changer," or forced humble-brag openers repeated across posts.
- Vary structure and length on purpose. Some posts are short observations (3 to 5 sentences), some are short personal stories (2 to 3 short paragraphs). Not every post is a structured essay with bullet points.
- Zero to three hashtags per post, most posts use zero or one. No hashtag walls.
- Never claim IRCC, TCF, or TEF endorsement. Never guarantee test scores. Never give specific immigration or legal advice, all commentary stays in French-learning and product territory, and immigration policy questions are explicitly left to licensed consultants (RCICs).

## How to use

Copy the `text` field directly into LinkedIn for the scheduled date and time. The `notes` field is for internal reference only and should not be posted.
