# ParleSprint v3 iPad App Store screenshots

The UI inside each framed screen is preserved from the six real iPad captures in `~/Downloads/v3-store-ipad-10.5 inch/`; only proportional resizing, a white rounded frame, and the surrounding headline/caption artwork are added.

The first four screenshots put TCF Canada, the four exam skills, the daily practice loop, and the Bonjour vocabulary lesson first. The last two show a real vocabulary check and a real story screen; they do not claim to be TCF listening, reading-exam, or writing-model questions.

The pale blue background was generated with the built-in imagegen tool to match the iPhone campaign artwork. The app captures were not generated or retouched. The composite script is `build_ipad_screenshots.py`; originals are copied unchanged to `source/ipad-10.5-inch-originals/`.
